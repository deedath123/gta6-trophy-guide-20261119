⚠ One trophy may be missable

Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at every spot across Leonida. PowerPyx reports one meditation is offered by Lucia on her balcony in Chapter 1 only, the morning after the mission Down to Earth, and it is gone once Chapter 1 ends. It is not confirmed whether the trophy needs that spot — but do it before Chapter 1 ends, just in case. Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter: there is no mission replay at launch. Everything else can be done in free roam after the story.

Platinum Path — session planner

Our one distinguishing hook: the 55 trophies sequenced into story-chapter-ordered play sessions. Work the sessions in order and the tracker tells you what is next.

Roadmap to the platinum

  1. Stage 1 — Story (Chapters 1–4 + finale). Play the story, do the stranger strands as they appear, and take the Chapter 1 meditation for Positive Mental Attitude. Story and mission trophies (17) pop as you play.
  2. Stage 2 — Duo & robbery trophies. Once Jason and Lucia are both playable (Chapter 2), do the partner trophies — Hot Together, Retail Therapy — and the robberies that open in Chapters 3–4: armored trucks, bank vaults, jewelry stores, gang operations.
  3. Stage 3 — Activities & counters. Shooting Range gold (42), 250 stunts, 20 races, 14 drone-race wins, fishing vs Cal, arcade, bowling, 50 challenges.
  4. Stage 4 — Collectibles & exploration. 3 card sets (15 of 18 for 100%), 100 WAiNK It locations, 100 vehicles, all 52 Leonida areas, 8 garages, 10 Scooter Bros registrations, 18 meditation spots.
  5. Stage 5 — 100% & clean-up. Finish the 100% completion checklist (A Long Road, gold), then sweep any counter left over. The platinum pops with the last trophy.

Counts that decide the grind: 397 stunts exist (250 needed), 42 Shooting Range challenges, 18 card sets, 18 meditation spots, 52 areas, 9 gangs, 80 challenges (70 for 100%), 277 vehicles (200 for 100%). Sources: Push Square trophy guide; PowerPyx via GTA Zone.
